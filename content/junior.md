UHCTF Junior is a spin-off of UHCTF for younger audiences. Over the years we have made small efforts to make security and CTFs accessible to younger people: children, teenagers and families. We are now making this material public, so that anyone can reuse it.

Everything lives in one open repository: **[github.com/uhctf/uhctf-jr](https://github.com/uhctf/uhctf-jr)**.

## What you'll find

- **Challenges**: reusable challenges, one folder each, with a README and a solution. Many work on paper or offline in the browser, and need little to no prior knowledge.
- **Events**: complete event formats that pick challenges and describe the planning, materials and scoring.

| Event | Audience |
| --- | --- |
| [Dag van de Wetenschap](https://github.com/uhctf/uhctf-jr/tree/main/events/dag-van-de-wetenschap) | families, children and teenagers |
| [DFL workshop](https://github.com/uhctf/uhctf-jr/tree/main/events/dfl-workshop) | last years of secondary school (17–18) |
| [TALim workshop](https://github.com/uhctf/uhctf-jr/tree/main/events/talim-workshop) | 5th grade of primary school (10–11) |

The material is mainly in Dutch, and some parts are also available in English.
